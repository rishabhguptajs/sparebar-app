## Content pillars

1. **Demo reels.** Screen record the studio: pick a character, type a scene, get the result. Under 20 seconds.
2. **Same face, many scenes.** One character in five settings in one reel. This is the product's whole point.
3. **Model showdowns.** Same prompt on two models (Seedance 2.0 vs Kling 3, Nano Banana 2.1 vs Seedream 5 Pro). Show the credit cost on screen.
4. **Cost reality.** "What 100 credits makes" and "an AI UGC ad for under 50 credits".
5. **Tips.** Prompt patterns for phone-shot looks, 9:16 framing, photo tips for characters.

## 20 reel ideas

Each is a hook (first 2 seconds, on screen as text), then the beat list.

1. **"I made 5 videos of one person without filming anything."** Character card, five scenes cut quickly, studio shot at the end.
2. **"Same face, five scenes."** Split screen of five stills, all the same character.

10. **"Which model for which job."** Three picks: Seedance 2.0 for people and motion, Veo 3.1 Fast for speech, Nano Banana 2.1 for cheap images.
11. **"Failed generation? You get the credits back."** Short trust reel with the refund line.
12. **"9:16 vs 16:9: pick before you generate."** Same scene both ways.
13. **"One character, a whole week of posts."** Seven stills as a content calendar.
14. **"Skincare unboxing with an AI creator."** The demo prompt from the site, end result.
15. **"Cheap vs premium model, same prompt."** Cost on screen: 2 credits vs 27 credits.

## Posting plan

- 4 reels a week for the first month, 1 story a day (screen record, polls).
- Post at the same times. Check Insights after two weeks and move to the slots with the best reach.
- Pin your three best reels: the same-face reel, the model showdown, the free-credits reel.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour. Follow and comment on UGC, ecommerce and AI creator accounts for 15 minutes a day.
